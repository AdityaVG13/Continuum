# 2026-10-26: veth+TBF queue calibration (omarchy)

Host: x86_64 Linux (Omarchy 4.0.6), unprivileged user namespaces.
Harness: `lab/veth_bloat.py` + `lab/udp_load.py`, run as
`unshare -Urn python3 lab/veth_bloat.py`.
Topology: two netns joined by veth; rtr egress capped at 20mbit via TBF
(burst 32kbit, latency 400ms); UDP load rtr->cli; ping RTT measured cli->rtr.

## What it proves

- Deep FIFO (pfifo limit 1000) behind a saturated 20mbit cap: ping RTT
  goes from ~0.03ms idle to ~36ms average (~43ms max). Over 1000x inflation.
  This is the bufferbloat shape: below saturation nothing queues, past it
  everything does.
- fq_codel under the same saturating load: ~0.3ms average (~0.5ms max).
  Roughly 100x better than FIFO, ~10x above its own idle baseline.
- Run-to-run stability: two full runs agree within 1ms on every saturated row.

## What it means for `src/sim.goose`

The sim's queue model (FIFO delay grows with the square of load, fair queue
stays near flat) matches the measured SHAPE. Absolute scale differs on
purpose: the lab's veth baseline is ~0.03ms, the sim's baseline is 25ms
(home-router domain). Two honest caveats:

1. The sim's quadratic acts earlier than real FIFO (at half load the sim
   already reports bloat; the real FIFO queues nothing until saturation).
   That makes the daemon conservative: it reaches for the fair profile
   before a real queue would force it. Acceptable, documented, not hidden.
2. The sim's fair profile (12ms over baseline at saturation) is flatter than
   measured fq_codel (~10x its own baseline). Same direction, kinder numbers.
