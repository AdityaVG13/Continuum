# Participant proof

## Destination

Show, with an independent readback, that the model applies the owned profile or abstains from the measurements. The demo may claim only what that readback shows. The router proof stays a labelled actuator, not the model's choice.

## Notes

Domain: a closed loop where the model is a participant and the guardian is the authority.

Standing constraints, already decided in the build, not closed tickets:

- The mechanical six-tool walk spends no model call. It is the dispatch proof.
- `participant_context` currently includes the task summaries "interactive evidence fetch" and "bulk transfer", and the catalogue id `sqm-fair`. It does not contain `apply_profile`. A pass on those strings can be prose-matching.
- A recorded reply and a live reply share `dispatch_sim_tool`. The loop does not substitute a profile.
- A live call runs only when `MODEL_API_KEY` is set. Absence of the key is not a pass.
- `--loop` with a key decides on the sim. `ROUTER_HOST` runs the scripted router leg beside it.
- Credentials stay in the environment. They do not enter the prompt.
- This repo has no issue-tracker doc. The map is local markdown. No `research/` branches: the repo forbids branches.
- Skills named by this effort that are not installed: grilling, domain-modeling. Do not invent the human's side of a grilling ticket.
